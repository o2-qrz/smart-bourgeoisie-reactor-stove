# 🏎 Smart-Bourgeoisie (Resilience-Stove) ### *Two-Chamber Automated Off-Grid Reactor with Quantum Air Injection and Cascade Ignition* **Project Overview:** This repository hosts the open-source engineering blueprints, matrix calculations, and automation logic for a high-efficiency, dual-chamber, grate-less wood stove. Co-developed as a 50/50 Human-AI agile sprint between the Lead Hardware Architect and Google Gemini AI, this system provides safe, continuous, and micro-regulated overnight heating without human intervention or reliance on the 220V power grid. --- ## 📊 Thermal Power & Fuel Efficiency Matrix Calculated for premium, high-density RUF wood briquettes (~0.83 kg per piece, 4.8 kWh/kg energy density) running inside our optimized 80% efficiency stove structure. | Parameter | Configuration: **3 + 3 Briquettes** (Standard Room) | Configuration: **6 + 6 Briquettes** (Heavy Duty Hub) | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Total Fuel Weight** | 5.0 kg total (2.5 kg per chamber) | 10.0 kg total (5.0 kg per chamber) | | **Gross Thermal Energy** | 24.0 kWh | 48.0 kWh | | **Net Useful Heat (80% Efficiency)**| **19.2 kWh** | **38.4 kWh** | | **Active Burn Time per Chamber** | 3.5 – 4.0 hours | 4.0 – 4.5 hours | | **Total Autonomous Cycle Runtime** | **approx. 8 – 10 hours** (inc. cool-down) | **approx. 10 – 12 hours** (inc. cool-down) | | **Average Thermal Power Output** | **~2.2 kW / hour** (Perfect for 15-22 m² spaces) | **~4.0 kW / hour** (Ideal for 35-45 m² zones) | --- ## 📐 Blueprint Anatomy & Structural Nodes (Based on Engineering Sketch) The internal layout utilizes a heavy-gauge, non-deforming cylindrical body divided into two structurally isolated processing chambers (**A** and **B**). ### 🚪 Node 1: Dual Hermetic Loading Interface * **The Mechanism:** Two independent, narrow vertical doors (**Chamber A** and **Chamber B**) mounted on a unified rigid structural frame. * **The Physics:** The edge of the active door features a heavy-duty steel overlapping sealing pressure bar (latch/naщельник) lined with high-temperature graphite stove gaskets. The minimal opening width limits air turbulence, creating a high-velocity localized vacuum that ensures zero smoke blow-back into the room during refuelling. ### 📐 Node 2 & 4: The V-Shaped Fusion Hearth (Grate-less Design) * **The Design:** Cast-iron grates are replaced by dual internal steel plates welded at a strict **30–35° angle** using 5mm carbon structural steel. * **The Physics:** Under gravity, fuel logs and briquettes slide into a flat 5-7cm central canal (**Node 4**). * **The Forge Perforations:** Lateral air injection holes (6–8mm diameter) are drilled 3cm above the base. Combustion air jets directly into the base of the embers from the sides, creating a highly concentrated, vortex-driven forge effect that burns fuel completely to fine ash. ### 💨 Node 3, 5 & 6: The Counter-Flow Air Shield & Isolator * **The Architecture:** The back profile reveals the main structural separator. A hollow **1.5–2 cm air jacket (Node 5)** splits the firebox in half. * **The Air Path:** Independent dual outdoor intake ports (**Node 6**) channel cold outside air up through this jacket. This creates an automated thermal firewall, preventing the standby chamber from reaching premature pyrolysis or log-ignition temperatures. * **The Common Flue Connection (Node 7):** Both chambers channel exhaust gas up into a single, shared flue chimney (**Node 7**). To eliminate the dependency on complex mechanical flue-switching dampers (which jam from creosote soot), this architecture maintains a direct open connection to the flue block. Back-drafting into the standby room is mathematically prevented by maintaining an airtight seal on the standby chamber's loading door (**Node 1**) and intake valve (**Node 6**). --- ## ⚡ The 12V Automation Circuit & Ignition Elements * **The Ignition Module:** A heavy-gauge nichrome heating loop is integrated directly into the flat base (**Node 4**) of Chamber B. It sits inside a ceramic protective sheath, in direct contact with an uncompressed natural birch bark fiber fuse or paraffin-treated wood rool. * **Pulse Air Intake Valves:** Two 12V automotive electromagnetic actuators (or motorized ball valves) control the ports (**Node 6**). They operate in an intermittent quantum-pulse pattern to feed oxygen in controlled bursts, preventing thermal spikes and extending the burn cycle. --- ## 💻 Arduino C++ Core Firmware (Open Architecture Sketch) *This code is provided for localized testing and open-source modernization.
